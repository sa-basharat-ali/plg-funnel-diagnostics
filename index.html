What surprised me

I expected account-level routing to be the big win. It sent 158 more accounts to sales for 7 more paid companies in 60 days. The unmapped form and the usage score mattered far more.

The part I got wrong

My first version's self-report matched the truth almost exactly, because I'd written it as the truth plus missing answers. Adding real recall mix-ups made it useful but biased, which is the case for reconciling sources.

What I'd do in the pod

DAYS 1–30

Count the assembly line

  • Reconcile every signup to one state, daily; find unmapped forms and sources.
  • Agree funnel stage definitions with Analytics Engineering and GTM Engineering.
  • Report the funnel at signup, workspace and company grain.
DAYS 31–60

Route on behavior

  • Build the Actions-velocity score and test it against the form-based rule.
  • Resolve personal-email signups to companies through shared workspaces.
  • Alert on routing drops and stalled stages in Hex or Sigma.
DAYS 61–90

Give credit honestly

  • One channel view from last-touch, self-report and affiliate links, with confidence.
  • Control-metro designs for the next events and billboards.
  • Day-10 agency cap alerts for customer success; ground truth to Strategic Finance.
